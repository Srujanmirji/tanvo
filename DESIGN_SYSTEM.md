# TANVO Design System

## Visual direction

Premium editorial design combined with cinematic WebGL.

References in spirit:

- luxury technology
- experimental creative agencies
- cinematic product launches
- modern editorial websites
- high-end WebGL experiences

Do not copy any specific website.

## Colors

Primary background:
#050505

Secondary background:
#090D14

Deep navy:
#0B1424

Warm white:
#F4F1EA

Muted text:
#858585

Electric violet:
#A98BFF

Champagne gold:
#C9A56A

Borders:
rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.12)

Use violet as the primary digital accent.

Use gold sparingly for emotional emphasis and key moments.

## Typography

Use a modern grotesk or contemporary sans-serif.

Headlines should be large and editorial.

Use italic serif only for occasional emphasis such as:

IMAGINE.
TO EXIST.

Recommended hierarchy:

Hero:
clamp(4rem, 8vw, 8rem)

Section heading:
clamp(3rem, 6vw, 6rem)

Large display:
clamp(4rem, 10vw, 10rem)

Body:
16 to 20px

Labels:
10 to 13px with increased letter spacing.

Avoid excessive font weights.

## Layout

Desktop max width:
1400px

Desktop side padding:
60 to 80px

Use a 12-column grid.

Prefer asymmetric layouts.

Do not center every section.

Use full-width visual environments.

## Shape language

Prefer:

- sharp geometry
- thin borders
- subtle circles
- architectural forms
- restrained radius

Avoid:

- excessive rounded cards
- bubbly UI
- cartoon illustrations

## Buttons

Primary button:

dark or light solid surface depending on section.

Use compact uppercase typography.

Arrow should animate independently.

Hover:

- subtle translation
- arrow movement
- border or background transition

## Cursor

Desktop only.

Default:
small dot.

Interactive:
expand to a small circle.

Project hover:
display VIEW PROJECT.

CTA hover:
display LET'S BUILD.

Disable on touch devices and reduced-motion mode.

## Texture

Use subtle:

- film grain
- atmospheric haze
- soft noise
- vignette

Do not make the page look dirty or heavily filtered.

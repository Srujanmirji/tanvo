06 FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how we work, what we build, and who we build it for.

Where is TANVO based?

TANVO is a digital strategy, design and development studio based in Hubli–Dharwad, Karnataka, India. We partner with regional and global founders, and work with clients well beyond Karnataka.

What services does TANVO offer?

Five capabilities: web design and development, headless e-commerce flagships, UI/UX design and prototyping, brand identity and design systems, and custom web applications and SaaS platforms. Most engagements combine several of these rather than one in isolation.

What technologies do you build with?

Next.js and React for web platforms, headless CMS architectures, and Tailwind CSS for styling. E-commerce work is built on Shopify Plus with custom headless storefronts, custom cart and checkout, ERP and inventory sync, and multi-currency support. Design work runs through Figma design systems, and we build to WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards.

What is your design and development process?

A four-stage framework. Discover & Immerse — discovery workshops, telemetry audits and journey mapping. Define & Strategize — turning findings into an architecture and plan. Design & Prototype — interactive prototypes and systematic design libraries. Deliver & Scale — build, launch and ongoing scale.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Our published work spans architectural real estate (Buy N Build — a 3D spatial configurator platform with CRM automation), furniture e-commerce (The Slow House — a headless Shopify storefront), and heritage jewellery (Shivakrupa — a craftsmanship archive with private consultation booking). The common thread is brands that need both considered design and real engineering.

How do I start a project, and how quickly will you reply?

Use the “Start a project” form on this page and tell us which capabilities you need plus a short brief. You can also email support@tanvo.in or call +91 96633 41218 or +91 80736 23745. We reply within 24 hours.

# TANVO Content and Copy Rules

## Brand voice

TANVO should sound:

- confident
- concise
- ambitious
- human
- technically capable

Avoid corporate filler.

Avoid phrases such as:

- cutting-edge solutions
- innovative solutions for your business
- one-stop solution
- best-in-class
- digital transformation partner

Prefer short, direct statements.

## Core copy

WE BUILD WHAT YOU IMAGINE.

YOUR DREAM DESERVES TO EXIST.

## CTA language

Primary:

START YOUR PROJECT ↗

Secondary:

LET'S BUILD IT.

OR JUST SAY HELLO

## Project content

Use fictional placeholder projects until real case studies are supplied.

Do not claim fictional projects are real.

Do not fabricate client names.

## Statistics

All metrics are placeholders.

Never present them as verified TANVO facts without actual data.

## Content architecture

Store copy in data files where possible.

Do not scatter repeated strings across components.
